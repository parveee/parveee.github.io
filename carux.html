Drive Select

UX Design Case Study

DriveSelect is a responsive mobile app designed for daily car rentals that enables users to conveniently search, compare, and book vehicles tailored to their travel needs. Built around a streamlined core structure—an Explore Home Screen for setting location and viewing deals, a Car Listings Page with smart filters for price and vehicle specifications, a Vehicle Details Page featuring clear itemized pricing, and a Checkout & Confirmation screen with multi-payment options and pickup maps—the project involved developing end-to-end user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes for MVP validation.

Core Strategy

Problems

  • Unclear Final Pricing – Hidden charges and surprise taxes make users drop out during payment.
  • Confusing Vehicle Lists – Browsing through different car types, seating capacities, and transmissions takes too much time.
  • Rigid Payment Methods – Lack of common digital payment options causes frustration at checkout.
  • Vague Pickup Locations – Renters struggle to find where to collect their booked vehicle.

    • Design Process

  • Research – Analyzed user rental habits and pain points.
  • Define – Set clear user goals for pricing transparency, instant filtering, and multi-gateway mobile checkout.
  • Wireframing – Then I create layouts of the screens to visualize the apps structure.
  • UI Design – After that I design interfaces that are easy to use and look clean.
  • Prototyping – Finally I build interactive flows for testing.

    • Solution

  • Location-Based Hero Search – Integrated quick auto-location detection and prominent promotional discounts right on the home screen.
  • Smart Filters and Clear Specifications – Added quick filters (Price, Car Type, Fuel/Transmission) along with clear seating and luggage badges.
  • Transparent Price Breakdown – Itemized daily rates, basic insurance, service fees, and final totals clearly before confirmation.
  • Instant Confirmation and Location Pin – Designed a clean success screen showing rental dates, total paid, and exact pickup terminal maps.

    • User Research & Goals

    Target Audience

  • Local Residents – Seek hassle-free digital verification, low security deposits, and flexible bookings for quick trips.
  • Tourists – Prioritize travel freedom, seamless airport options, and transparent pricing with zero hidden fees.
  • Business Travelers – Demand guaranteed vehicle availability, clean cars, and instant digital invoicing for fast expense claims.

    • Goals

  • Simplify Car Discovery – Make searching, filtering, and picking an affordable car quick and hassle-free.
  • Provide Full Price Transparency – Display exact itemized costs before forcing the user into checkout.
  • Streamline Mobile Booking – Offer a simple booking process with local digital wallet integration.
  • Offer Clear Pickup Guidance – Provide precise location details and simple booking confirmations.

    • User Research Insights

  • Insight 1 – Users strongly prefer a fully digital onboarding process where identity and driving license verification happen instantly.
  • Insight 2 – Clear, transparent pricing with no hidden charges or unexpected damage penalties is the biggest trust factor for customers choosing a rental app.
  • Insight 3 – Flexible pick-up and drop-off locations, especially around airports and key city hubs, significantly increase booking completion rates.
