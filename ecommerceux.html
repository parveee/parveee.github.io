foodiva

UX Design Case Study

Foodiva is a responsive e-commerce platform designed for grocery shopping, enabling users to conveniently browse and purchase daily essentials online. The project involved creating end-to-end user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes for MVP validation through four key pages: a Homepage featuring daily essential deals, popular categories, and brand showcases; a Products Page offering complete product listings with search and instant add-to-cart capabilities; an About Us Page highlighting the brand story, core values, team, and customer reviews; and a Contact Us Page equipped with direct communication channels, a inquiry form, location details, and an FAQ section.

Core Strategy

Problems

  • Inconvenient Grocery Shopping -People lack time to physically visit markets for daily needs.
  • Delayed Delivery Options -Traditional shopping methods lack fast.
  • Lack of Price Clarity - struggle to find active discounts, offers, and best deals.

    • Design Process

  • Research – Analyzed online and physical grocery shopping behaviors, pain points, and target audience needs.
  • Define – Outlined core user goals, key requirements, and essential platform pages.
  • Ideate – Sketched layouts and user flows for four key pages.
  • Design – Sketched layouts, user flows for four key pages and interactive prototypes.

    • Solution

  • Seamless E-Commerce Platform – Designed a clean, intuitive grocery shopping website for quick navigation.
  • Fast Delivery – clear fast-delivery.
  • Accessible Support System – Provided multi-channel contact options (WhatsApp, Phone, Email) and FAQs for trust.
  • Real-Time Order Tracking & Management – Provided live location.

    • User Research & Goals

    Target Audience

  • Busy Professionals – Limited time for physically visiting stores.
  • Delayed Delivery Options – Regularly purchase fresh produce and daily essentials.
  • Digital-Savvy Seniors – Value simple interfaces and direct doorstep delivery.

    • Goals

  • Simplify Grocery Shopping– convenient online platform for purchasing fresh produce.
  • Ensure Effortless Product Discovery – Enable quick browsing through clear categories.
  • Enable Real-Time Order Tracking – Allow users to monitor their order details.
  • Build Customer Trust & Support – Deliver reliable customer care through accessible support channels.

    • User Research Insights

  • Insight 1 – Users struggle to quickly find specific dietary or regional brands they trust, highlighting the need for prominent brand showcases and easy filtering.
  • Insight 2 Users are discouraged by slow delivery times for perishable items, demanding faster and reliable doorstep service.
  • Insight 3 – Navigating diverse product in categories
