foodiva

UX Design Case Study

Foodiva is a responsive e-commerce platform designed for grocery shopping, enabling users to conveniently browse and purchase daily essentials online. The project involved creating end-to-end user flows, wireframes, and interactive prototypes for MVP validation through four key pages: a Homepage featuring daily essential deals, popular categories, and brand showcases; a Products Page offering complete product listings with search and instant add-to-cart capabilities; an About Us Page highlighting the brand story, core values, team, and customer reviews; and a Contact Us Page equipped with direct communication channels, a inquiry form, location details, and an FAQ section.
